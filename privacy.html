Snapshot Solitaire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Snapshot Solitaire (the “Game”) is a free mobile game for Android developed by AI pirates Studio (“we”, “us”). The Game is free because it shows ads. This policy explains what information the Game and the services built into it collect, why, and what choices you have.

Summary

  • The Game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay together with Google AdMob, Unity Ads and Liftoff. These partners use your device’s advertising ID and other device information to show and measure ads.
  • We use Google Firebase (analytics, crash reports and remote settings), Tenjin (ad revenue and install measurement) and our own analytics server to understand how the Game is played and to keep it working.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before ads are personalized and before our analytics and Tenjin start. In US states with privacy laws, the Game shows a privacy notice where those laws require it.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, boosters and settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.

Advertising

The Game shows banner, full-screen (interstitial) and optional rewarded video ads. They are delivered by the Unity LevelPlay mediation platform (by Unity Technologies, formerly ironSource), which picks an ad from these networks:

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle)

To show, cap, measure and prevent fraud in ads, these SDKs collect information from your device, such as the Android advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language, app version, ad interactions (views, clicks, rewards) and approximate location derived from the IP address. Each partner processes this information under its own privacy policy (links below).

Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. On first launch the Game shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, using the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Until you answer, nothing is treated as allowed. Your answer is passed to LevelPlay, and ad networks that read the consent signal apply it.
  • US states with privacy laws. Where required, the Game shows a notice through the same Google platform that lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. Partners that support this signal receive your choice.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu depends on your device).

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Google Firebase, provided by Google:

  • Firebase Analytics receives only three events from the Game: the first launch after install, each app start, and each ad impression with its ad revenue. Firebase also records device and app information and an app instance ID. The Game passes your consent choices to Firebase (Google Consent Mode).
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error reports (device model, operating system, app version and the state of the app at the time of the error) so we can fix problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads game settings from Google’s servers so we can adjust the Game without an update.

Install and ad revenue measurement (Tenjin)

We use Tenjin to measure how many people install the Game, from which advertising campaigns, and how much ad revenue the Game earns. The Tenjin SDK collects the advertising ID, App Set ID, Google Play install referrer, IP address and device information, and the Game sends it the revenue of each ad impression. Tenjin may pass install and revenue results to the ad networks we use to promote the Game. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Tenjin starts only if you allow storage and measurement in the consent form; elsewhere it starts when the Game opens.

Our own game analytics

To understand how the Game is played and to balance its levels, the Game sends gameplay events to our own analytics server: for example starting, winning, quitting or restarting a level, tutorial steps, use and receipt of boosters and coins, offers shown and taken, ad requests and results (including ad revenue), daily streaks, settings changes and your consent choice. Each event comes with:

  • a random install ID created by the Game (not your advertising ID and not linked to your name or account);
  • app version, device model and Android version;
  • the country and language set on your device (taken from the device settings, not from your IP address).

Data is sent over HTTPS to a server we run on DigitalOcean in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. The server does not store IP addresses. Events are deleted automatically 730 days after they were recorded.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, events are held on the device until you answer the consent form and are sent only if you allow analytics storage. If you refuse, the waiting events and the install ID are deleted from your device and nothing is sent. Outside these regions, events are sent once the Game has checked whether consent is required.

What we do not do

  • We do not use the Facebook / Meta SDK.
  • We do not send your gameplay events to Google Ads or to ad networks. Ad networks receive data only through their own SDKs described above and through Tenjin’s install and revenue results.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

Changing your choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you can make a new choice by clearing the Game’s data in Android Settings → Apps → Snapshot Solitaire → Storage → Clear data; the consent form will appear again on the next launch. Note that this also deletes your game progress. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings, as described above.

Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Our analytics server deletes events automatically after 730 days.
  • Firebase, Tenjin and the ad partners keep data according to their own policies and settings.

Our server data is tied only to a random install ID, not to your name, email or advertising ID, so in most cases we cannot identify which records belong to you. If you want to ask about, access or delete your data, write to us and we will help as far as we are able to identify it. For data held by Google, Unity, Liftoff or Tenjin, you can also use the tools described in their privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or under US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The Game is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has used the Game, please contact us.

International transfers and security

Our partners may process information in the United States and other countries. Information that the Game sends to us and to our partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
